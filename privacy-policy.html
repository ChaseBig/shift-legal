Privacy Policy

Shift · Last updated October 16, 2026

Shift is a breathing-exercise app built to work without an account. This page describes, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

There's no account, and no server of ours

Shift doesn't ask you to sign up, sign in, or create a profile. There's no backend of ours that your data is sent to. Everything the app remembers (your chosen goals, techniques, session history, streaks, reminders, and settings) is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it. We never see it. Two things do leave your device, both described below: a feedback message, only if you choose to send one, and the purchase check that keeps Shift Pro working.

Your name, if you choose to give one

During setup, Shift optionally asks for a first name so it can personalize a greeting ("Good morning, Alex"). This is entirely optional, stored only on your device, and never leaves it. Skipping it doesn't limit anything in the app.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health sync in Settings, Shift writes a Mindful Minutes entry to Health each time you complete a session. On iOS 18 or later, Shift also asks "How do you feel right now?" after a session. Answering is optional. If you pick an answer, Shift saves it to Health once, as a State of Mind entry. Writing these two kinds of entries is the only thing Shift does with Health. Shift never requests permission to read anything from Health, and it doesn't send what it writes anywhere else. Apple's Health app stores those entries; Shift doesn't keep a copy. You can disconnect this at any time from Settings or from the Health app's own privacy controls, and any future writes stop immediately.

Notifications

Reminder notifications, if you set any up, are scheduled entirely on your device using the operating system's own local notification system. Nothing about your reminder schedule is sent anywhere.

Feedback

If you send feedback from within Shift, the message you type is submitted to Formspree, a form-delivery service we use to receive it. Formspree forwards it to us; we don't use it for anything besides reading and responding to your feedback. See Formspree's own privacy policy for how they handle it. You aren't asked for your name or email to submit feedback.

Subscriptions

Shift Pro is an optional subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store and processed by RevenueCat, the subscription infrastructure provider we use to validate purchases and keep your Pro status in sync if you reinstall or switch devices. RevenueCat receives only what's necessary to do that (a purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier for your install) — never your name, contacts, or anything else Shift stores locally. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy and Apple's for how each of them handles that.

What Shift doesn't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks — Shift has never included one.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or renting of your data, and no sharing beyond the two services named above (Formspree for feedback, RevenueCat for purchases), each only for the purpose described.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: kept until you remove it. Settings → Delete history removes your session history, streaks, and breath-hold records; Settings → Reset app to defaults clears goals, techniques, and settings; deleting the app removes everything Shift stored.
  • Feedback messages: kept in our inbox only as long as needed to read and respond to them. Email us to have a message deleted.
  • Purchase records: RevenueCat keeps your purchase receipt and the anonymous ID so it can restore your access; its privacy policy says how long. Email us to have that record deleted. Apple keeps its own purchase history under its policy.
  • Apple Health: Mindful Minutes and State of Mind entries Shift wrote live in the Health app, where you can delete them.

Formspree and RevenueCat receive only the data described on this page and use it only to deliver feedback and confirm purchases for us. We rely on them to protect it at least as well as this policy does.

Children

Shift isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone in that age group. Apart from a feedback message someone chooses to send and the anonymous purchase ID, nothing leaves the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature needed to send data somewhere it doesn't today — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
