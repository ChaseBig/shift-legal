Privacy Policy

Shift · Last updated July 23, 2026

Shift is a breathing-exercise app built to work without an account. This page describes, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

There's no account, and no server

Shift doesn't ask you to sign up, sign in, or create a profile. There's no backend of ours that your data is sent to. Everything the app remembers — your chosen goals, techniques, session history, streaks, reminders, and settings — is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it. We never see it. The one exception, if you ever use it, is sending feedback — see below.

Your name, if you choose to give one

During setup, Shift optionally asks for a first name so it can personalize a greeting ("Good morning, Alex"). This is entirely optional, stored only on your device, and never leaves it. Skipping it doesn't limit anything in the app.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health sync in Settings, Shift writes a Mindful Minutes entry to Health each time you complete a session. That's the only thing it does with Health — Shift never requests permission to read anything from Health, and doesn't ask for it. You can disconnect this at any time from Settings or from the Health app's own privacy controls, and any future writes stop immediately.

Notifications

Reminder notifications, if you set any up, are scheduled entirely on your device using the operating system's own local notification system. Nothing about your reminder schedule is sent anywhere.

Feedback

If you send feedback from within Shift, the message you type is submitted to Formspree, a form-delivery service we use to receive it — that's the one deliberate outbound message this app sends. Formspree forwards it to us; we don't use it for anything besides reading and responding to your feedback. See Formspree's own privacy policy for how they handle it. You aren't asked for your name or email to submit feedback.

Subscriptions

Shift Pro is an optional subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store and processed by RevenueCat, the subscription infrastructure provider we use to validate purchases and keep your Pro status in sync if you reinstall or switch devices. RevenueCat receives only what's necessary to do that (a purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier for your install) — never your name, contacts, or anything else Shift stores locally. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy and Apple's for how each of them handles that.

What Shift doesn't do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks — Shift has never included one.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with anyone, for any reason — there isn't any of yours on a server to share.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Children

Shift isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone in that age group — practically, since nothing is collected off-device at all, there's nothing to knowingly collect from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature needed to send data somewhere it doesn't today — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how Shift handles information can be sent to chase.small@outlook.com, or via Settings → Send feedback in the app.
