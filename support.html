Support

Shift · Last updated October 16, 2026

Contact

Email chase.small@outlook.com, or use Settings → Send feedback in the app. The in-app form doesn't ask for your email, so include it in the message if you'd like a reply.

Restore a purchase

Open Shift, tap any feature marked PRO to open the Shift Pro screen, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Shift Pro with.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Shift Pro. Monthly and annual plans renew automatically until you cancel; the lifetime purchase never renews.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Free premium-audio sessions

New installs include two free sessions with ambience and voice guidance. One is used the moment premium audio starts playing in a session. After that, the sound library is part of Shift Pro; the free tone stays free.

Your data

Your sessions, history, and settings stay on your device. Settings → Delete history removes your session history, streaks, and breath-hold records. Deleting the app removes everything Shift stored. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Apple Health

Shift only writes to Health, and only if you turn sync on in Settings → Apple Health. It saves each completed session as Mindful Minutes. On iOS 18 or later, it also asks how you feel after a session, and if you answer, it saves that as a State of Mind entry. To stop, turn sync off in Settings → Apple Health, or change Shift's access in the Health app's privacy settings.
