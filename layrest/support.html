Restore a purchase

Open Layrest, tap the gear icon, then tap Restore purchases. The Layrest Pro screen has the same button. Use the same account you bought Layrest Pro with: your Apple Account on iPhone, or your Google Account on Android.

Cancel a subscription

  • iPhone: subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Layrest Pro.
  • Android: subscriptions are managed by Google Play. Open your subscriptions in Google Play, select Layrest Pro, and tap Cancel subscription. Uninstalling Layrest doesn't cancel it. Google's steps: Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play.

Monthly and annual plans renew automatically until you cancel. The lifetime purchase never renews.

Refunds

  • iPhone: Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: Google Play handles refunds for Google Play purchases. On play.google.com, open your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history, and choose Report a problem next to the order. Google's steps: Request a refund on Google Play.

Free Pro nights

New installs include two free nights with Pro sounds. A night starts when you press play on a mix that has a Pro sound or a Pro pulse, and it lasts 12 hours, so pausing in the middle of the night doesn't use another one. After both nights, the seven free sounds stay free.

Sleep timer

The timer counts playback time, so pausing also pauses the timer. Near the end, the sound fades out slowly and then stops. Pressing play again starts the full timer over.

Sound stops with the screen locked

Layrest keeps playing with the screen locked. Sound stops if another app starts playing audio, a call comes in, or headphones disconnect. Press play on the lock screen or in the app to start it again.

Apple Watch

The watch app is a remote for Layrest on your iPhone. It plays and pauses, loads saved mixes and sets the timer, and the sound plays from your iPhone. There's no watch app for Android.

Apple Health

Apple Health is on iPhone only; the Android app doesn't have it. Layrest only reads sleep data that your Apple Watch or iPhone records, and only after you tap Connect Apple Health on the Sleep screen. It never writes to Health. To turn access off, open the Health app, tap your profile, then Apps → Layrest.

Your data

Your mixes, saved mixes, timer and play history stay on your device. Deleting the app removes everything Layrest stored on your phone, on iPhone and on Android. See the Privacy Policy for details.
