Layrest: Sleep Sounds

Layer gentle rain over ocean waves and brown noise, set a timer, and let it fade out as you fall asleep.

Download on the App Store

Make your own sleep mix

  • Rain, ocean waves, fan, thunderstorm, brown noise, white noise and more.
  • Layer several sounds and set each one's volume.
  • A sleep timer that fades the sound out gently, or type in your own time.
  • Save your favorite mixes with Layrest Pro.
  • An Apple Watch remote for what's playing.
  • No account or sign-in.

Free and Pro

  • Free: 7 sounds, 2 sounds layered at once, timers up to 6 hr 45 min.
  • Layrest Pro: all 42 sounds, up to 5 layered sounds, longer or no timers, saved mixes and extended Apple Health sleep history.

Questions

Does Layrest need an account?
No. There's no account or sign-in.
Can I play more than one sound?
Yes. Free users can layer 2 sounds, and Pro users up to 5.
Does the sound keep playing all night?
Free timers run up to 6 hours 45 minutes. Pro adds longer timers or no timer at all.

Layrest is for relaxation. It is not a medical device.
