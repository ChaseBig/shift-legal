What Layrest is

Layrest plays recorded and generated sounds, including noise colors, binaural beats and an optional rhythmic "pulse" in volume. It's a relaxation tool, not a medical device, and isn't intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, including sleep disorders or tinnitus. Talk to a doctor about ongoing sleep problems.

Keep the volume at a comfortable, low level, especially with headphones and when listening for long periods or overnight. If you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, check with a doctor before using binaural beats or the pulse feature, and stop using any sound that makes you feel unwell.
