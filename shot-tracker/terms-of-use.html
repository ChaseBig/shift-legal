Talk to your clinician

Always follow the instructions of your prescriber and pharmacist. Do not start, stop, or change a medication or dose based on anything shown in this app. If you have severe or worrying symptoms, contact your clinician or emergency services right away.

About the medication level estimate

The level chart is a mathematical estimate built from the half-life and time-to-peak published in each medication’s FDA label and from the doses you log. Real levels vary from person to person. It is not a lab measurement and must not be used to decide when or how much to take.

About the protein target

The default target uses 1.2 g of protein per kg of goal weight. A 2025 joint advisory from the American College of Lifestyle Medicine, the American Society for Nutrition, the Obesity Medicine Association and The Obesity Society notes that 1.2–1.6 g/kg/day has been proposed during active weight reduction (Mozaffarian et al., Obesity 2025;33:1475-1503). The same advisory recommends individual counseling for people with kidney problems or a history of kidney stones, so confirm the right target with your care team.
