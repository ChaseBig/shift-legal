What Shot Tracker is

Shot Tracker is a personal tracking tool for people taking GLP-1 medications. It records what you enter and shows summaries and estimates. It doesn't diagnose, treat or recommend doses, and it isn't a medical device.

Medical disclaimer

Shot Tracker doesn't give medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your prescriber and pharmacist. Don't start, stop or change a medication or dose based on anything shown in the app. If you have severe or worrying symptoms, contact your clinician or emergency services right away.

The medication level chart is a mathematical estimate built from the half-life and time to peak published in each medication's FDA label and from the doses you log. Actual levels vary from person to person. It isn't a lab measurement and must not be used to decide when or how much to take.

The default protein target uses 1.2 g of protein per kg of goal weight. A 2025 joint advisory from the American College of Lifestyle Medicine, the American Society for Nutrition, the Obesity Medicine Association and The Obesity Society notes that 1.2 to 1.6 g/kg/day has been proposed during active weight reduction (Mozaffarian et al., Obesity 2025;33:1475-1503). The same advisory recommends individual counseling for people with kidney problems or a history of kidney stones, so confirm the right target with your care team.
