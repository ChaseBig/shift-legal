Shot Tracker: GLP-1 shot log for iPhone and Apple Watch

A private log for people taking a GLP-1 medication such as semaglutide, tirzepatide or orforglipron. Log each dose and injection spot, see when your next shot is due, and keep your weight, protein and side effects in one place. Shot logging is free, and there's no sign-up or account to create.

Download on the App Store

What you can track

  • Doses and injection sites: a body map of injection spots that suggests the next spot, so you rotate sites.
  • Your next dose: a countdown for weekly, daily or every-few-days schedules, with a reminder on dose day and, for weekly doses, the evening before.
  • Weight: your trend, rate per week and progress toward your goal. Weigh-ins from a smart scale come in through Apple Health.
  • Protein: one-tap logging toward a daily target.
  • Side effects: log several at once and see which days after a dose they show up.
  • Supply: pens and vials on hand, with a reminder to reorder.
  • Reports: a PDF of your doses, weight and symptoms for an appointment, and a spreadsheet export.

On Apple Watch and your Lock Screen

The Watch app and complication count down to your next dose. With Premium you can log a dose from your wrist: pick the spot on a body diagram with the Digital Crown, check the dose and time, and confirm. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your next dose too.

Private by default

No sign-up and no account: no email, no password, nothing to log in to. No ads and no usage tracking. Your log stays on your iPhone and out of iCloud unless you send it to your Apple Watch, to Apple Health or to a file you export.

Questions

Is Shot Tracker free?
Yes. Dose logging, the countdown and reminders, site rotation, the countdown on widgets and Apple Watch, weight, side effects, Apple Health sync, the app lock and backups are free with no limit. Premium adds the medication level estimate, Apple Watch logging, protein, trends, photos, measurements, supply and reports, monthly, yearly (with a 7-day free trial) or as a one-time lifetime purchase.
Which medications does it work with?
GLP-1 medications including semaglutide, tirzepatide, orforglipron, dulaglutide and liraglutide, as injections or daily pills.
How is the medication level estimated?
From the doses you log and the half-life and time to peak in each medication's FDA label. It's an estimate, not a measurement.
Do I have to sign up or create an account?
No. You open the app and start logging. There's no sign-up, no email, no password and no account, and your log isn't stored on a server.
Can I move my log to a new phone?
Yes. Export a backup file in Settings, then restore it from the Welcome screen on the new phone. You can also import past doses from a spreadsheet (CSV).
Does it tell me what dose to take?
No. Shot Tracker doesn't calculate or recommend doses. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.

Shot Tracker is a tracking tool. It isn't a medical device, and it doesn't diagnose, treat or recommend doses. It isn't affiliated with any drug manufacturer.
