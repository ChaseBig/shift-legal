Privacy Policy

Shot Tracker (GLP Shot Tracker on the App Store) · Last updated October 28, 2026

Shot Tracker is built so that your health information stays on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and the choices you have.

What we store

Everything you enter (medication plan, doses, injection sites, weight, body measurements, protein, side effects, supplies and notes) is stored only in the app’s storage on your device. Shot Tracker has no account system and no server that receives this information.

Progress photos are copied into the app’s private folder on your device. They are not added to your photo library, are not included in backup files, and are deleted when you delete all data or remove the app.

When you scan a medication carton or pharmacy label, Shot Tracker reads the barcode and printed text in the photo on your iPhone with Apple’s Vision framework. The photo is not uploaded, and the app deletes its copy as soon as it has been read. A photo you chose from your library stays in your library. You check what was found before anything is saved.

If your device backs up app data to iCloud or Google, that backup is controlled by your device settings and the backup provider, not by Shot Tracker.

What leaves your device

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. If you subscribe, the store and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, Inc., receive purchase information (a random app user ID, product, price, and transaction dates) so your subscription can be verified and restored. They do not receive your health entries.

When you export a PDF report, a spreadsheet or a backup file, the file goes only where you send it from the share sheet.

When you contact support from the app, your email app opens a message to us with what you wrote, the name you gave (if any), the reply address you entered, and the app version, device model, operating system and your subscription status, so we can help. Nothing is sent until you send that email. The "Contact support" button on the error screen sends only the error message.

Apple Health

Apple Health sync is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, Shot Tracker reads body weight from Apple Health so weigh-ins from your scale or other apps appear in your progress, and writes the weigh-ins and protein you log in Shot Tracker to Apple Health. You choose which of these are allowed in the permission sheet, and you can change that in the Health app at any time.

Information from Apple Health stays on your device inside Shot Tracker. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone, including RevenueCat. Turning sync off stops reading and writing. Entries already written to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Widgets and Apple Watch

With Premium, Shot Tracker writes a short summary (your next dose time and amount, estimated level, protein today, latest weight, your last five doses, and your injection spots with when each was last used) to storage shared only with its own widgets, and sends the same summary to the Shot Tracker app on your paired Apple Watch through Apple’s Watch Connectivity. Doses (with the spot and amount), protein and symptoms you log on the Watch are sent back to the app on your iPhone the same way.

If you add a Shot Tracker widget to your Lock Screen or a complication to a watch face, anyone who can see that screen can read it. Remove the widget or complication to stop showing it.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, show ads, use advertising identifiers, run third-party analytics, or send your entries to any AI service.

Your choices

You can export all of your data as a backup file at any time from Settings. You can delete all of your data from Settings, which removes it from the app immediately. Deleting the app also removes its data from the device.

Children

Shot Tracker is intended for adults aged 18 and over.
