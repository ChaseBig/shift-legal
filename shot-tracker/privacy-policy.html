Privacy Policy

Shot Tracker (GLP Shot Tracker on the App Store) · Last updated November 4, 2026

Shot Tracker is built so that your health information stays on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

What we store

Everything you enter (medication plan, doses, injection sites, weight, body measurements, protein, side effects, supplies and notes) is stored only in the app’s storage on your device. Shot Tracker has no account system and no server that receives this information.

Progress photos are copied into the app’s private folder on your device. They are not added to your photo library, are not included in backup files you export, and are deleted when you delete all data or remove the app.

Shot Tracker doesn’t store your information in iCloud. Your log and your progress photos are kept out of iCloud Backup and other device backups, so they stay on your iPhone.

What leaves your device

Purchases are processed by the App Store. Each time Shot Tracker opens, it contacts our subscription provider, RevenueCat, Inc., to load prices and check whether you have Premium. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your device type and operating system, and the time you last used the app, and, when you buy or restore, your App Store receipt with the product, price and transaction dates. We use this to provide and restore Premium, and to see totals across all customers, such as how many trials become subscriptions. RevenueCat never receives your health entries.

RevenueCat processes this information for us under a data processing agreement that requires it to protect the information at least as well as this policy does. RevenueCat’s privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

When you export a PDF report, a spreadsheet, a backup file or a progress image, the file goes only where you send it from the share sheet. A progress image shows a percentage or a count, never your weight, medication or doses.

When you contact support from the app, your email app opens a message to us with what you wrote, the name and reply address you gave (both optional), and the app version, device model, operating system, your subscription status and your support ID (the random ID RevenueCat uses for your purchases), so we can help. Nothing is sent until you send that email. The "Contact support" button on the error screen sends only the error message.

Apple Health

Apple Health sync is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, Shot Tracker reads body weight from Apple Health so weigh-ins from your scale or other apps appear in your progress, and writes the weigh-ins and protein you log in Shot Tracker to Apple Health. You choose which of these are allowed in the permission sheet, and you can change that in the Health app at any time.

Information from Apple Health stays on your device inside Shot Tracker. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone, including RevenueCat. Turning sync off stops reading and writing. Entries already written to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Widgets and Apple Watch

Without Premium, Shot Tracker writes only the time of your next dose, whether it is due or overdue, and how often you take it to storage shared only with its own widgets, and sends the same to the Shot Tracker app on your paired Apple Watch, so the free countdown can show it.

With Premium, Shot Tracker writes a short summary (your next dose time and amount, estimated level, protein today, latest weight, your last five doses, and your injection spots with when each was last used) to storage shared only with its own widgets, and sends the same summary to the Shot Tracker app on your paired Apple Watch through Apple’s Watch Connectivity. Doses (with the spot and amount), protein and symptoms you log on the Watch are sent back to the app on your iPhone the same way.

If you add a Shot Tracker widget to your Lock Screen or Home Screen, or a complication to a watch face, anyone who can see that screen can read it. Remove the widget or complication to stop showing it.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, show ads, use advertising identifiers, track you across other apps or websites, or send your entries to any AI service. Apart from the subscription totals RevenueCat shows us, Shot Tracker has no analytics.

How long data is kept

Your entries and progress photos stay on your iPhone until you delete them, delete all data in Settings, or remove the app.

RevenueCat keeps purchase records while they are needed to provide subscriptions and to meet legal obligations. To have yours deleted, email us from Settings > Contact support. The message includes the support ID we need to find your record, and we delete it within 30 days.

Support emails are kept until your question is answered, and deleted within 12 months.

Your choices

You can export all of your data as a backup file at any time from Settings. You can delete all of your data from Settings, which removes it from the app immediately. Deleting the app also removes its data from the device.

You can turn Apple Health sync off in Settings at any time, and change or withdraw its access in the Health app. Notifications, camera and Face ID access can be changed in your iPhone’s Settings.

Children

Shot Tracker is intended for adults aged 18 and over.
