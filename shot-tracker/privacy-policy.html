Privacy Policy

Shot Tracker (GLP Shot Tracker on the App Store) · Last updated October 28, 2026

Shot Tracker is a private tracker for people taking GLP-1 medications. It runs on iPhone, with an Apple Watch app and widgets, and on Android. It's built so that your health information stays on your device. This page explains what the app stores, what can leave your device, and the choices you have. Where something differs between iPhone and Android, the section says so.

There's no account, and no server of ours

Shot Tracker doesn't ask you to sign up or sign in. There's no server of ours that receives your entries. Everything you enter is stored only in the app's storage on your device: your medication plan, doses, injection sites, weight, body measurements, protein, side effects, supplies, notes and settings. We never see it.

Progress photos are copied into the app's private folder on your device. They aren't added to your photo library, aren't included in Shot Tracker backup files, and are deleted when you delete all data in the app or remove the app.

If your phone backs up its apps (iCloud Backup on iPhone, or Google's backup on Android, if you've turned it on), that backup can include Shot Tracker's data. The backup is controlled by your device settings and your Apple or Google account, and we can't see it.

What can leave your device

Only these things leave your device, and each one is described below:

  • Purchases. Shot Tracker Premium is bought through the App Store on iPhone or Google Play on Android. The store and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, Inc., receive purchase information (a random app user ID, the product, the price and the transaction dates) so your purchase can be checked and restored. They don't receive your health entries.
  • Files you export. When you export a doctor report (PDF), a spreadsheet (CSV) or a backup file, the file goes only where you send it from the share sheet.
  • Support emails. When you contact support from the app, your email app opens a message to us. It contains what you write, the name and reply address you give, your app version, device model, operating system version, and whether you have Premium. Your health entries are never included. You can read the whole message before you send it.
  • Apple Health and Apple Watch, if you use them. Both are described in their own sections below.

Apple Health (iPhone only)

Apple Health sync is part of Premium and is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it's on, Shot Tracker reads body weight from Apple Health, so weigh-ins from your scale or other apps appear in your progress. It writes the weigh-ins and protein you log in Shot Tracker to Apple Health. It reads nothing else from Health. You choose which of these are allowed in Apple's permission sheet, and you can change that at any time in the Health app: tap your profile, then Apps, then Shot Tracker.

Information from Apple Health stays on your device inside Shot Tracker. It's never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone, including RevenueCat. Weigh-ins that came from Apple Health are never written back to it. Turning sync off stops reading and writing. Entries already written to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. The Android app has no Health integration.

Widgets and Apple Watch (iPhone only)

With Premium, Shot Tracker writes a short summary (your next dose time, estimated medication level, protein today, latest weight and next injection spot) to storage that only its own widgets can read. It sends the same summary to the Shot Tracker app on your paired Apple Watch through Apple's Watch Connectivity. Doses and protein you log on the Watch are sent back to Shot Tracker on your iPhone the same way. Nothing is sent to a server of ours. Without Premium, the widgets and the Watch receive no health data.

If you add a Shot Tracker widget to your Lock Screen or a complication to a watch face, anyone who can see that screen can read it. Remove the widget or complication to stop showing it.

Face ID and app lock

With Premium, you can lock Shot Tracker with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode (on Android, your fingerprint, face or screen lock). The check is done by your phone's operating system. Shot Tracker only learns whether it succeeded and never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode data.

Reminders

Dose, protein, weigh-in, refill and free-trial reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system. There's no push server, and nothing about your reminders is sent anywhere.

Camera and photos

Shot Tracker uses the camera only when you take a progress photo, and opens your photo library only when you choose one. It never uses the microphone.

What Shot Tracker doesn't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No AI. Your entries are never sent to an AI service.
  • No selling or renting of your data.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Keeping and deleting your data

On your device: kept until you remove it. You can export a backup file at any time from Settings. Settings > Delete all data removes every entry and photo from the app immediately, and deleting the app removes its data from the device.

Phone backups: iCloud Backup and Google's backup follow your Apple or Google account settings. You can delete a backup there.

Support emails: kept in our inbox only as long as needed to answer them. Email us to have a message deleted.

Purchase records: RevenueCat keeps the purchase receipt and the random app user ID so it can restore your access. See RevenueCat's privacy policy, Apple's and Google's. Email us to have the RevenueCat record deleted.

Children

Shot Tracker is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future feature needs to send data somewhere it doesn't today, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
