Support

Shot Tracker (GLP Shot Tracker on the App Store) · Last updated November 3, 2026

Contact

Email shift.layrest.dev@gmail.com, or open Shot Tracker and tap Settings > Contact support. The in-app form adds your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and support ID for you. Tell me what you were doing when the problem happened, and I'll reply as soon as I can. Please don't send health details you'd rather keep private; I don't need them to help.

Restore a purchase

Open Shot Tracker and tap Settings > Restore purchases. The Premium screen has the same button. Use the Apple Account you bought Premium with.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Shot Tracker, or tap Manage subscription in Shot Tracker's Settings. Deleting Shot Tracker doesn't cancel a subscription.

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged. The lifetime purchase never renews. If notifications are allowed when your free trial starts, Shot Tracker reminds you two days before it ends.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders

Turn reminders on or off in Settings > Reminders. If they don't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Shot Tracker in your iPhone's Settings app.

Apple Watch

The Watch app counts down to your next dose for free. With Premium it also shows your estimated level and protein today, and you can log a dose at the suggested injection spot, protein and symptoms from your wrist. It needs watchOS 10 or later. To install it, open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install next to Shot Tracker. To add a complication, touch and hold your watch face, tap Edit, swipe to the complications and choose Shot Tracker.

Entries you make on the Watch reach your iPhone the next time Shot Tracker opens there.

Widgets

The Next dose widget is free and counts down to your next dose. With Premium it also shows your estimated level, and the Protein today widget shows your protein so far against your target. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Shot Tracker. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, then tap the area under the clock.

Apple Health

Turn on Settings > Apple Health. Shot Tracker reads body weight from Apple Health and writes the weigh-ins and protein you log. To change access later, open the Health app, tap your profile, then Apps > Shot Tracker.

App lock

Open Settings > App lock and turn on Lock Shot Tracker. It then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode each time you return to it.

If someone else knows your iPhone passcode, choose Shot Tracker passcode instead and pick 4 digits only you know. Your iPhone passcode no longer opens the app, and Face ID can still unlock it if you allow that. After five wrong passcodes the keypad pauses, for longer each time. If you forget the passcode, tap Forgot passcode? and reset it with Face ID or Touch ID. Without either, the only way back in is to delete Shot Tracker's data on the phone, because there's no account to recover it with.

Move to a new iPhone

Your entries live only on your iPhone and are kept out of iCloud Backup, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone doesn't bring them across. Make a backup file before you switch: tap Settings > Export backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new iPhone, such as Files or email. On the new iPhone, install Shot Tracker and tap Restore from a backup file on the Welcome screen, then restore your purchase in Settings. Progress photos aren't included in backup files.

Coming from another tracker? Settings > Import from a spreadsheet (CSV) adds doses and weigh-ins from a file with a Date column and a Dose (mg) or Weight column.

Delete your data

Settings > Delete all data removes every dose, weigh-in, protein entry, symptom and photo from the app. It can't be undone. Anything already written to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app. Deleting the app also removes its data. To have your purchase record deleted from our subscription provider, email us from Settings > Contact support and we'll delete it within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.
