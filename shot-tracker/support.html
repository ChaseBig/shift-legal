Support

Shot Tracker (GLP Shot Tracker on the App Store) · Last updated October 28, 2026

Contact

Email shift.layrest.dev@gmail.com, or open Shot Tracker and tap Settings > Contact support. Tell me your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened, and I'll reply as soon as I can. Please don't send health details you'd rather keep private; I don't need them to help.

Restore a purchase

Open Shot Tracker and tap Settings > Restore purchases. The Premium screen has the same button. Use the same account you bought Premium with: your Apple Account on iPhone, or your Google Account on Android.

Cancel a subscription

  • iPhone: subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Shot Tracker, or tap Manage subscription in Shot Tracker's Settings.
  • Android: subscriptions are managed by Google Play. Open your subscriptions in Google Play, select Shot Tracker, and tap Cancel subscription. Uninstalling Shot Tracker doesn't cancel it.

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged. The lifetime purchase never renews. When a free trial starts, Shot Tracker schedules a reminder for two days before it ends.

Refunds

  • iPhone: Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: Google Play handles refunds. See Request a refund on Google Play.

Reminders

Turn reminders on or off in Settings > Reminders. If they don't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Shot Tracker in your phone's Settings app.

Apple Watch (iPhone only)

The Watch app shows your countdown to the next dose, your estimated level and your protein today. You can log a dose at the suggested injection spot and add protein from your wrist. It needs Premium and watchOS 10 or later. To install it, open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install next to Shot Tracker. To add a complication, touch and hold your watch face, tap Edit, swipe to the complications and choose Shot Tracker.

Entries you make on the Watch reach your iPhone the next time Shot Tracker opens there.

Widgets (iPhone only)

With Premium, Shot Tracker has Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for your level and next dose, and for protein today. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Shot Tracker. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, then tap the area under the clock.

Apple Health (iPhone only)

With Premium, turn on Settings > Apple Health. Shot Tracker reads body weight from Apple Health and writes the weigh-ins and protein you log. To change access later, open the Health app, tap your profile, then Apps > Shot Tracker.

Face ID lock

With Premium, turn on Settings > App lock. Shot Tracker then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode each time you return to it.

Move to a new phone

Your entries live only on your phone, so make a backup before you switch. Tap Settings > Export backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as Files or email. On the new phone, install Shot Tracker, go through setup and restore your purchase, then tap Settings > Import backup. Importing replaces everything on the new phone with the backup. Progress photos aren't included in backup files.

Delete your data

Settings > Delete all data removes every dose, weigh-in, protein entry, symptom and photo from the app. It can't be undone. Anything already written to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app. Deleting the app also removes its data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
