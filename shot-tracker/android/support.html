Support

Shot Tracker · Last updated November 3, 2026

Contact

Email shift.layrest.dev@gmail.com, or open Shot Tracker and tap Settings > Contact support. The in-app form adds your app version, phone model, Android version and support ID for you. Tell me what you were doing when the problem happened, and I'll reply as soon as I can. Please don't send health details you'd rather keep private; I don't need them to help.

Restore a purchase

Open Shot Tracker and tap Settings > Restore purchases. The Premium screen has the same button. Use the Google account you bought Premium with.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Google Play. Open your subscriptions in Google Play, select Shot Tracker and tap Cancel subscription, or tap Manage subscription in Shot Tracker's Settings. Uninstalling Shot Tracker doesn't cancel a subscription.

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged. The lifetime purchase never renews. If notifications are allowed when your free trial starts, Shot Tracker reminds you two days before it ends.

Refunds

Google Play handles refunds. See Request a refund on Google Play.

Reminders

Turn reminders on or off in Settings > Reminders. If they don't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Shot Tracker in your phone's settings. On Android 14 and later, also allow Alarms & reminders so dose reminders arrive on time.

Home screen widget

The Next dose widget is free and counts down to your next dose. With Premium it also shows your estimated level. Add it from Settings in Shot Tracker, or touch and hold your home screen, tap Widgets and choose Shot Tracker.

Health Connect

Turn on Settings > Health Connect. Shot Tracker reads body weight from Health Connect and writes the weigh-ins and protein you log. On Android 9 to 13, install Health Connect from Google Play first. To change access later, open Health Connect and choose Shot Tracker.

App lock

Open Settings > App lock and choose how to unlock: your fingerprint, face unlock or screen lock, or a 4-digit Shot Tracker passcode that only you know. While the lock is on, Shot Tracker is hidden in recent apps and screenshots of it are blocked.

With a Shot Tracker passcode, your phone's screen lock no longer opens the app. After five wrong passcodes the keypad pauses, for longer each time. If you forget the passcode, tap Forgot passcode? and reset it with your fingerprint or face. Without either, the only way back in is to delete Shot Tracker's data on the phone, because there's no account to recover it with.

Move to a new phone

Your entries live only on your phone, so save a backup file before you switch: tap Settings > Export backup and save the file to your Drive, Files or an email. On the new phone, install Shot Tracker and tap Restore from a backup file on the Welcome screen. Progress photos aren't included in backup files.

Coming from another tracker? Settings > Import from a spreadsheet (CSV) adds doses and weigh-ins from a file with a Date column and a Dose (mg) or Weight column.

Delete your data

Settings > Delete all data removes every dose, weigh-in, protein entry, symptom and photo from the app. It can't be undone. Anything already written to Health Connect stays there until you delete it in Health Connect. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. To have your purchase record deleted from our subscription provider, email us from Settings > Contact support and we'll delete it within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.
