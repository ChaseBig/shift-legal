Privacy Policy

Shot Tracker · Last updated November 4, 2026

Shot Tracker is built so that your health information stays on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

What we store

Everything you enter (medication plan, doses, injection sites, weight, body measurements, protein, side effects, supplies and notes) is stored only in the app’s storage on your device. Shot Tracker has no account system and no server that receives this information.

Progress photos are copied into the app’s private folder on your device. They are not added to your photo library, are not included in backup files you export, and are deleted when you delete all data or remove the app.

If your phone backs up app data to Google, that backup is controlled by your phone’s settings and by Google, not by Shot Tracker.

What leaves your device

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Each time Shot Tracker opens, it contacts our subscription provider, RevenueCat, Inc., to load prices and check whether you have Premium. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your device type and operating system, and the time you last used the app, and, when you buy or restore, your Google Play purchase token with the product, price and transaction dates. We use this to provide and restore Premium, and to see totals across all customers, such as how many trials become subscriptions. RevenueCat never receives your health entries.

RevenueCat processes this information for us under a data processing agreement that requires it to protect the information at least as well as this policy does. RevenueCat’s privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

When you export a PDF report, a spreadsheet, a backup file or a progress image, the file goes only where you send it from the share sheet. A progress image shows a percentage or a count, never your weight, medication or doses.

When you contact support from the app, your email app opens a message to us with what you wrote, the name and reply address you gave (both optional), and the app version, device model, operating system, your subscription status and your support ID (the random ID RevenueCat uses for your purchases), so we can help. Nothing is sent until you send that email. The "Contact support" button on the error screen sends only the error message.

Health Connect

Health Connect sync is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, Shot Tracker reads body weight from Health Connect so weigh-ins from your scale or other apps appear in your progress, and writes the weigh-ins and protein you log in Shot Tracker to Health Connect, as weight and nutrition. You choose which of these are allowed on the Health Connect permission screen, and you can change that in Health Connect at any time.

Information from Health Connect stays on your device inside Shot Tracker. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone, including RevenueCat. Turning sync off stops reading and writing. Entries already written to Health Connect stay there until you delete them in Health Connect.

Home screen widget

Shot Tracker keeps what its home screen widget shows in the app’s private storage on your phone. Without Premium, that is only the time of your next dose, whether it is due or overdue, and how often you take it. With Premium, it is a short summary: your next dose time and amount, estimated level, protein today, latest weight, your last five doses, and your injection spots with when each was last used.

If you add a Shot Tracker widget to your home screen, anyone who can see that screen can read it. Remove the widget to stop showing it.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, show ads, use advertising identifiers, track you across other apps or websites, or send your entries to any AI service. Apart from the subscription totals RevenueCat shows us, Shot Tracker has no analytics.

How long data is kept

Your entries and progress photos stay on your phone until you delete them, delete all data in Settings, or remove the app.

RevenueCat keeps purchase records while they are needed to provide subscriptions and to meet legal obligations. To have yours deleted, email us from Settings > Contact support. The message includes the support ID we need to find your record, and we delete it within 30 days.

Support emails are kept until your question is answered, and deleted within 12 months.

Your choices

You can export all of your data as a backup file at any time from Settings. You can delete all of your data from Settings, which removes it from the app immediately. Deleting the app also removes its data from the device.

You can turn Health Connect sync off in Settings at any time, and change or withdraw its access in Health Connect. Notifications and camera access can be changed in your phone’s settings.

Children

Shot Tracker is intended for adults aged 18 and over.
